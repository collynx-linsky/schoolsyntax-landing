Pricing Questions

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. There are no penalties for changing plans.

What happens if I exceed my student limit?

We won't lock you out unexpectedly. When you approach 90% of your student limit, we'll notify you and offer upgrade options. You can also purchase additional student capacity as an add-on without changing your plan.

Do you offer educational discounts or NGO pricing?

Yes. We offer up to 40% discounts for registered non-profit educational institutions and government schools in developing nations. Contact our sales team with your institution's registration documentation to apply.

Is data migration included in the price?

Standard plan includes self-serve data import via CSV. Professional plan includes guided migration support. Enterprise plan includes full white-glove data migration by our technical team at no extra cost.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing is charged as a single payment upfront and saves you 20% compared to monthly billing. Your subscription renews automatically at the anniversary date. We send renewal reminders 30 and 7 days in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), M-Pesa, bank transfer (for annual Enterprise contracts), and PayPal. All payments are processed securely and you'll receive a VAT-compliant invoice.
