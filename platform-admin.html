Platform Administration

Total Platform Control in Your Hands

The Platform Admin dashboard gives network operators, SaaS resellers, and super administrators complete governance over every institution on the platform — without touching their operational data.

Admin Capabilities

Everything a platform operator needs to govern at scale

Institution Management

Create, configure, suspend, or delete institution tenants. View onboarding status, module enablement, and tenant health metrics from a central registry.

Subscription & Billing

Manage subscription plans, payment status, grace periods, and upgrade/downgrade flows. View revenue analytics, MRR, and churn data across all tenants.

Support Impersonation

Log in as an institution admin to diagnose support issues — with explicit activation, full audit logging, and automatic expiry. The institution can see all impersonation events.

Platform Broadcasts

Send announcements to all institutions, specific subscription tiers, or individual tenants. Schedule maintenance windows, product updates, or policy change notices platform-wide.

Domain Management

View and manage all custom domains across all institutions. Monitor SSL certificate status, domain DNS verification, and expiry dates from a central dashboard.

Platform Audit Logs

Full platform-level audit trail. Every admin action — tenant creation, subscription change, impersonation event — is logged with actor, timestamp, and full context.

System Monitoring

Platform-wide health metrics: active tenants, daily active users, storage utilization, SMS credit consumption, error rates, and infrastructure performance indicators.

Feature Flag Control

Enable or disable platform features per institution or per subscription tier. Roll out new features gradually with institution-level feature flags controlled from the admin dashboard.

Admin Dashboard

A command center for platform operators

The Platform Admin portal lives at /platform/ and is accessible only to users with the Platform Superadmin role. It's a fully separate interface from the institution-level admin, with its own access controls and audit trail.
