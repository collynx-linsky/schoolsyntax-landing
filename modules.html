25+ Integrated Modules

One platform. Every module your institution needs.

Every module is purpose-built for educational institutions, deeply integrated with each other, and available from day one. No third-party plugins. No integration headaches.

Module 01

Academic Management

The operational core of your institution. Manage every aspect of academic life — from session configuration to timetabling — with tools that automate the repetitive and surface the important.

  • Academic year, term & semester management
  • Class, stream & section configuration
  • Subject & curriculum management
  • Automated timetable generation & conflict detection
  • Teacher assignment & workload management
  • Holiday calendar & event scheduling
Structured academic management in action
Module 02

Admissions Management

Transform your intake process from a paperwork nightmare to a seamless digital experience. Parents apply online, your team reviews and communicates, all within a single workflow.

  • Online application portal with document uploads
  • Multi-stage application review workflow
  • Automated status notifications to applicants
  • One-click conversion from applicant to enrolled student
  • Admissions quota management per class
  • Enrollment trend analytics and reporting
Digital admissions — from application to enrolment
Module 03

Finance & Billing

From fee configuration to collection analytics, the Finance module gives your bursar complete control and visibility. Automate the billing cycle and drastically reduce fee defaults.

  • Multi-component fee structure configuration
  • Automated invoice generation & bulk billing
  • Payment tracking with multiple payment methods
  • Scholarship & bursary management
  • Automated payment reminder sequences
  • Financial reports: aging, collection summary, budget vs actual
Real-time fee collection and financial reporting
