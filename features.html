Platform Capabilities

Every feature a modern institution demands

SchoolSyntaxERP is not a collection of loosely-connected apps. It's a purpose-built, deeply integrated platform with enterprise-grade features in every dimension.

Academic Year Management

Define academic years, terms, semesters, and holiday calendars. Automatically apply configurations across all relevant modules.

Class & Stream Management

Create unlimited classes, streams, and sections. Assign teachers, students, subjects, and rooms with drag-and-drop simplicity.

Automated Timetabling

Generate conflict-free timetables automatically based on subjects, teacher availability, and classroom capacity. Export and share instantly.

Examination Management

Full exam lifecycle — scheduling, mark entry, grade calculation, result publication, and parent report generation — all automated.

Fee Structure Configuration

Create complex fee structures with multiple components, payment installment plans, discount tiers, and penalty rules — all without code.

Automated Invoice Generation

Invoices generated automatically at term start or on a schedule. Sent directly to parents via email and SMS. Fully branded with your institution's logo.

Payment Reminders

Automated SMS and email reminders for outstanding balances. Configurable reminder schedules. Escalating messaging for chronic non-payment.

Financial Reporting

Real-time collection summaries, aging reports, budget variance analysis, and term-over-term revenue trends. Export to PDF, Excel, or CSV.

Staff Records Management

Maintain complete employee profiles — personal details, qualifications, employment history, documents, performance records — in one secure database.

Automated Payroll

Process payroll in minutes, not days. Handles PAYE, NHIF, NSSF, allowances, deductions, and payslip distribution. Full audit trail on every run.

Leave Management

Self-service leave requests, multi-level approvals, leave balance tracking, and calendar integration. Full leave history per employee.

True Multi-Tenancy

Each institution is a completely isolated tenant. Separate data stores, separate configurations, separate domains. Zero data leakage by design.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permission system with roles (Super Admin, Principal, Bursar, Teacher, Parent, Student) and custom permission sets for every use case.

Custom Domains & SSL

Each institution gets a branded subdomain. Bring your own domain with automatic SSL provisioning. Full white-label capability for enterprise clients.

Full Audit Logging

Every action on the platform is logged with user, timestamp, IP address, and before/after values. Immutable audit trail for compliance and forensics.

Bulk SMS Campaigns

Send targeted SMS messages to filtered groups — all parents, specific classes, overdue fee payers, or absentees. Delivery receipts and analytics included.

Email Automation

Trigger-based email sequences for fee reminders, result releases, event invitations, and welcome messages. Beautifully branded HTML email templates.

Announcements & Noticeboard

Publish school-wide or targeted announcements to staff, students, or parents. Pinned notices on parent and student portals. Read-receipt tracking.

Executive Dashboards

Leadership-ready dashboards showing enrollment trends, fee collection rates, attendance summary, exam performance, and staff metrics — all real-time.

Custom Report Builder

Build any report from any data source with a drag-and-drop report builder. Schedule automatic delivery to your inbox in PDF, Excel, or CSV format.

Student Performance Analytics

Track individual and cohort-level academic performance over time. Identify at-risk students early with automated alerts to class teachers.
