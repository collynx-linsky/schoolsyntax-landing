Product Roadmap

Where we're going next

We build SchoolSyntaxERP in public. Here's what we've shipped, what's in progress, and what's coming. Your feedback shapes our roadmap.

Q1 2026 — Delivered

Platform Analytics & Reporting Overhaul

Complete rebuild of the analytics engine with real-time dashboards, custom report builder, and scheduled report delivery. Now supports drag-and-drop report creation without SQL knowledge.

Real-time Dashboards Custom Report Builder Scheduled Reports Export to Excel/PDF/CSV
Q2 2026 — In Progress

AI-Powered Attendance Intelligence

Machine learning models to identify attendance patterns, predict at-risk students, and automatically escalate chronic absenteeism to the right staff member before it becomes a crisis.

Attendance Prediction At-Risk Alerts Pattern Analysis
Q2 2026 — In Progress

Parent Mobile App (iOS & Android)

Native mobile app for parents to view attendance, results, fee balances, and school announcements in real-time. Push notifications for all critical events.

iOS App Android App Push Notifications Fee Payment In-App
Q3 2026 — Planned

AI Grading Assistant

AI-assisted grading suggestions for teachers based on historical grade distributions, peer performance, and learning objectives. Teachers review and approve — AI does the heavy lifting.

AI Grading Suggestions Grade Distribution Analysis Teacher Review Workflow
Q3 2026 — Planned

Library & Asset Management Module

Track school books, equipment, and assets. Manage lending, returns, and loss. Integrated with student profiles for full lifecycle asset management.

Book Catalogue Lending Management Asset Registry Barcode Scanning
Q4 2026 — Planned

Financial Forecasting & Budget Management

Predictive financial modeling based on enrollment projections, fee collection history, and expense patterns. Annual budget planning with variance tracking.

Budget Planning Module Revenue Forecasting Expense Tracking
